# MyArteLab Brand Voice Guide

**The Definitive UX Writing and Copy Style Standard for MyArteLab**  
*For Product Designers, Engineers, and AI Pair-Programmers*

---

## 1. The Non-Negotiable Brand Anchor

> ### **"Built for African creators"**
>
> This wording is established brand copy. Preserve it exactly, character for character, everywhere it currently appears.
> - Do not rewrite it.
> - Do not add words to it.
> - Do not remove words from it.
> - Do not change its capitalization or punctuation.
> - Do not replace it with a variation.
> - If it appears in multiple places, keep every existing occurrence unchanged.

---

## 2. Brand Personality

MyArteLab is the digital home and commissioning platform where African creators showcase their portfolios, collaborate with global clients, and get paid securely.

Our voice is:
1. **Warm and Human:** We speak like a creative collaborator who understands late-night render times, revision rounds, client feedback, and the relief of invoice payments clearing. We use natural language and natural contractions (*you're*, *we've*, *can't*, *that's*).
2. **Confident & Creative:** We take creative work seriously without taking ourselves too seriously. We speak with quiet authority about design, animation, music, photography, code, and illustration.
3. **Lightly Playful (Never Performing):** We celebrate milestones and invite exploration with genuine charm, but we never force jokes onto empty screens or turn serious payment errors into comedy sketches.
4. **Authentically African:** We are proud of African creative culture. We speak universally to creators across Lagos, Nairobi, Accra, Johannesburg, Cairo, and the diaspora. We never rely on caricature, forced pidgin, or monolithic stereotypes.
5. **Calm and Direct with Money:** When wallets, balances, transactions, and contracts are involved, clarity is our highest duty. We state outcomes plainly without ambiguity or false promises.

## 3. Do's and Don'ts

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Use sentence case for interface labels (*"Sign in"*, *"Create account"*, *"Recent activity"*). | Use Title Case everywhere (*"Sign In With Email"*, *"View All Recent Deliverables"*). |
| State exactly what happened and what to do next (*"We couldn't save that. Try again in a moment."*). | Use vague, helpless templates (*"Oops! Something went wrong. Please try again later."*). |
| Give buttons concrete action verbs (*"Save changes"*, *"Submit proposal"*, *"Withdraw"*). | Use generic, passive button labels (*"Submit"*, *"Proceed"*, *"Click here"*). |
| Respect user time with short, punchy sentences. | Pad sentences with corporate filler or fluffy marketing buzzwords. |
| Use contractions naturally (*"you'll"*, *"we've"*, *"don't"*). | Use stiff, bureaucratic phrasing (*"One must ensure that the user provides..."*). |
| Keep payment and status distinctions crystal clear (*"Requested"*, *"In escrow"*, *"Released"*). | Blur distinct financial states into generic "success" or "pending". |
| Celebrate genuine creative milestones warmly. | Put exclamation marks on every routine confirmation toast. |

## 4. Banned Phrases List (AI Clichés)

Never use the following generic marketing and chatbot expressions in newly authored or edited copy:

- ❌ *"Welcome back!"* → ✅ *"Good to see you."*
- ❌ *"Oops! Something went wrong"* → ✅ *"We couldn't load that right now."*
- ❌ *"Unlock your potential"* / *"Unlock the full experience"* → ✅ *"Set up your space to share work."*
- ❌ *"Elevate your craft"* / *"Elevate your experience"* → ✅ *"Showcase your creative work."*
- ❌ *"Seamlessly"* / *"Effortlessly"* → (Omit the adverb; describe the actual workflow).
- ❌ *"Revolutionize"* / *"Empower"* → (Describe what the user can actually accomplish).
- ❌ *"Get started today"* / *"Your journey starts here"* → ✅ *"Create your profile"* / *"Explore creators"*.
- ❌ *"Discover the power of..."* → (Say directly what the tool does).
- ❌ *"Let's dive in"* → ✅ *"Get started"*.
- ❌ *"No data found"* → ✅ *"Nothing here yet"* / *"No matches this time"*.
- ❌ *"Are you sure you want to proceed?"* → ✅ *"Ready to log out?"* / *"Cancel this booking?"*.
- ❌ *"Congratulations! Your request has been successfully processed."* → ✅ *"Request sent."*

## 5. Fixed Product Terminology Glossary

| Concept | Preferred Term | Avoid (Accidental Synonyms) | Context & Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Authentication** | **Sign in** / **Sign out** | Log In, Login, Sign On, Log Out | Use *"Sign in"* for the action; use *"Sign out"* for ending a session. |
| **New Account** | **Create account** / **Sign up** | Register, Join, Enroll | *"Create account"* is clear and unambiguous on buttons. |
| **User Role 1** | **Creator** | Freelancer, Artist, Vendor, Seller | The creative professional providing services. |
| **User Role 2** | **Client** | Buyer, Customer, Employer | The party commissioning or hiring creative talent. |
| **Engagement** | **Booking** | Order, Gig, Reservation | A structured engagement between a client and a creator. |
| **Proposal** | **Booking request** | Inquiry, Offer, Proposal | A pending booking initiated before acceptance and escrow funding. |
| **Deliverable** | **Deliverable** | Item, Output, Milestone file | An agreed output or file item submitted for client review. |
| **Escrow** | **Escrow** | Vault, Hold, Lockbox | The smart contract holding client funds until deliverable approval. |
| **Wallet balance** | **Available balance** | Total balance, Liquid cash | Funds currently available for withdrawal or booking escrow. |
| **Incoming funds** | **In escrow** / **Pending earnings** | Locked money | Funds awaiting deliverable approval or release. |
| **Adding funds** | **Add funds** | Deposit, Top-up, Fund account | Primary user action to add USDC or fiat into the wallet. |
| **Sending funds out** | **Withdraw** / **Payout** | Cash out, Offramp | Transferring funds to a local bank account or Solana address. |
| **Social Content** | **Post** | Service, Tweet, Update | A creative portfolio update published to the community feed. |
| **Community Feed** | **Feed** | Stream, Timeline, Explore feed | The central home feed of creator works and updates. |
| **Trust Status** | **Verified** / **Verified creator** | Certified, Authenticated | The official $1/month badge verifying creator identity. |

## 6. Tone by Context

| Screen / Situation | Target Tone | Key Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Routine Navigation** | Invisible, crisp, clean | Keep labels to 1–2 words. Prioritize scanability. |
| **Sign in & Auth** | Welcoming, low-friction | Get straight to the form. No motivational speeches. |
| **Onboarding** | Encouraging, conversational | Explain why details matter (*"so clients know how to reach you"*). |
| **Feed & Portfolios** | Creative, vibrant, appreciative | Focus on the artwork itself. Let creator visuals shine. |
| **Booking & Deliverables** | Professional, structured, calm | Exact clarity on status (*Requested*, *In progress*, *Delivered*, *Approved*). |
| **Wallet & Payouts** | Precise, reassuring, transparent | Never use jokes. State currency, fees, and timelines accurately. |
| **Success & Wins** | Satisfying, concise | One short sentence is enough (*"Changes saved."*, *"Payment sent."*). |
| **Errors & Recovery** | Honest, helpful, non-dramatic | State what failed and offer a genuine next step. |
| **Empty States** | Warm invitation | Explain what belongs here and how to start. |
| **Destructive Actions** | Clear, unambiguous | Specify what will be deleted or cancelled. Specific button verbs. |
| **Transactional Emails** | Human, structured | Key fact in the headline; details in a scannable table below. |

## 7. Ten Canonical Reference Patterns

1. **Sign in screen:**  
   *Before:* "Welcome back! We're excited to have you back. Sign in to unlock your creative potential."  
   *After:* **"Good to see you. Sign in to pick up where you left off."**
2. **Error toast:**  
   *Before:* "Oops! Something went wrong. Please try again later."  
   *After:* **"We couldn't save that. Try again in a moment."**
3. **Empty portfolio:**  
   *Before:* "No data found. Start adding content to your portfolio to showcase your work."  
   *After:* **"Your portfolio is looking a little quiet. Add your first piece of work to bring it to life."**
4. **Booking request sent:**  
   *Before:* "Congratulations! Your booking request has been successfully processed."  
   *After:* **"Booking request sent. We'll let you know when the creator responds."**
5. **Payment failure:**  
   *Before:* "Payment Failed! An error occurred while processing your transaction. Please try again."  
   *After:* **"We couldn't confirm this payment. Check your balance before trying again."**
6. **Sign out confirmation:**  
   *Before:* "Are you sure you want to proceed with logging out of your account?"  
   *After:* **"Ready to sign out? You can sign back in anytime."** (Buttons: *"Stay signed in"* / *"Sign out"*)
7. **Empty search results:**  
   *Before:* "No creators found. Try adjusting your search criteria."  
   *After:* **"No matches this time. Try another search and see who's out there."**
8. **Save confirmation:**  
   *Before:* "Success! Your changes have been saved successfully."  
   *After:* **"Changes saved."**
9. **Form validation:**  
   *Before:* "Validation Error: This field is required."  
   *After:* **"Add a project title to continue."**
10. **No booking activity:**  
    *Before:* "You have no bookings at this time. Get started today to discover new opportunities."  
    *After:* **"No bookings yet. When a client books you, you'll see it here."**
